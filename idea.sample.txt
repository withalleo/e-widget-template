## What I want to build (one or two sentences)

A live team poll. People on the board tap one of several answer options and
see the vote tally update in real time as a bar chart.


## Who uses it and why

Workshop facilitators and their participants. During a meeting the facilitator
asks a question, everyone votes from their own device, and the group watches
the results fill in live.


## Main features / how it should behave

- Show a question at the top and 2–6 answer options as big, tappable buttons.
- Each tap adds one vote to that option and updates a horizontal bar chart with
  counts and percentages.
- One vote per person; tapping a different option moves that person's vote.
- Everyone viewing the board sees the same numbers at the same time.


## Look and feel

- Dark background, light text, one accent color for the bars and buttons.
- Minimal and flat — no heavy borders or shadows.
- Big touch targets; it will be used on tablets and touchscreens.
- Let board editors change the background, accent, and text colors.


## Collaboration and saved state

- Keep it in sync for everyone: the question, the options, and the vote
  counts should be shared across all copies of the widget on the board.
- When someone opens the board later, they should see the current tally, not a
  blank poll.


## Actions

- When someone votes, tell the board which option was chosen (so other board
  automations can react).
- Allow the board to reset the poll or jump to a specific question from outside
  the widget.


## Data and external services

- No external data. It is fully self-contained and needs no internet access,
  API keys, or accounts.


## Size

- Roughly 900 × 600 to start, but it should still look fine if resized larger
  or smaller.


## Anything else / things I'm unsure about

- The default question and options can be placeholders — I'll edit them later
  in the configuration section.
- If one vote per person is hard to guarantee, a simple per-widget vote is fine;
  just note the limitation.
